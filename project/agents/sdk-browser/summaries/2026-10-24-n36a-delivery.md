## Done

- **Delivery layer** `sdk/browser/src/transport/`. Every ingest request (OTLP traces and logs, replay chunks) goes through one `Delivery`:
  - `retry.ts`: retry on network errors, timeouts, 408, 429 and 5xx; exponential backoff with equal jitter, `Retry-After` honoured; 5 attempts by default. Other 4xx answers are dropped and counted.
  - `gzip.ts`: `CompressionStream` gzip for bodies of 1 KB or more.
  - `keepalive.ts`: one shared keepalive budget (60 KB, 9 requests) for OTLP and replay.
  - `delivery.ts`, the page-hide path: no await before fetch, no gzip, keepalive within the budget. Beyond it, `sendBeacon` (opt-in) or the offline store plus a plain request. A retry waiting for its backoff is woken at page hide.
  - `store.ts`: IndexedDB `ouo-delivery` with a hard byte cap (5 MB default) and oldest-first eviction in one transaction, entries older than 24 h dropped. Memory fallback when storage is missing, blocked, private, hangs or is full; nothing throws into the page. Drained on `online`, on show, after a success and on the next page load, one tab at a time (Web Locks).
  - `exporter.ts`: OTLP JSON exporter (`@opentelemetry/otlp-transformer` serializers). It replaces `OTLPTraceExporter` / `OTLPLogExporter`; at page hide it splits a batch to fit the keepalive budget.
  - `rum.deliveryStats()`: sent, retried, queued, dropped with reasons, bytes before and after gzip. Every drop is logged with `debug: true`, including export-queue overflows (`Batcher` `onDrop`).
- `telemetry/batch.ts`: queue overflow is reported (`onDrop`); the comment points at the delivery layer. The page-hide flushes in `index.ts` (#50) are unchanged, and the delivery layer works under them.
- `replay/uploader.ts` sends through `Delivery`. `chunker.ts` gets one change: a refused chunk (`retryable: false`) is not retried.
- `ingest.ts`: a relative `endpoint` / `replayEndpoint` (`/otel`) resolves against the page origin.
- `index.ts` / `types.ts`: wiring only: `transport` options, `deliveryStats()`, `delivery.close()` in `_uninstall`, exporters from `transport/`.
- First-party path:
  - `docs/first-party-endpoint.md`: nginx, Caddy, Next.js and Vite routes; rate limiting behind a proxy; beacons through a proxy that adds the credential.
  - `examples/ouo-web/scripts/serve-first-party.mjs` and `run.sh --first-party`.
  - README sections "Delivery" and "First-party endpoint".
- Bundle: the delivery layer adds about 1.2 KB gzip. After the rebase onto N36 B (#72, terser), index.js is 43.9 KB against the unchanged 44.0 budget, so the budget is kept. The headroom is only about 0.1 KB.
- Checklist: N44 added (beacons without a proxy need a collector-side decision, ADR-0007 amendment).
