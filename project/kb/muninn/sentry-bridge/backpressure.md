# Back-pressure of the bridge with Muninn and LGTM

Measured 2026-11-15 with `deploy/scripts/sentry-backpressure.sh` on an ouo-env `--muninn` stack (numbers in the [report](../../../agents/sentry-demo/reports/bridge-muninn-lgtm.html#pressure)).

- **The SDK's answer never waits on a backend.** The bridge answers (or forwards to Sentry and copies its answer) first, then hands the envelope to a bounded queue with a non-blocking send; translation runs on its own workers. With Loki, Muninn or rum-api stopped, 600 envelopes per phase all got 200, p99 under 200 ms, the same as the baseline within the noise of a loaded Mac.
- **What survives an outage.** Loki, Tempo, Mimir and the `ouoreplay` exporter queue in memory (1 000 batches, retry 5 min): a 1 to 2 minute outage lost nothing (40 / 40 sessions in Loki, 40 / 40 video replays in rum-api afterwards); a longer one, or a collector restart during it, loses data. Only Muninn's spec-wire queue is on disk: 200 chunks of 256 KiB (54 MB) queued while Muninn was stopped, all stored 15 s after it came back. Collector memory stayed under 100 MiB throughout.
- **The rate limiter is the real risk.** Translated output is charged to `ouoratelimit` with the client address the bridge saw and no request metadata, so `key_from: x_forwarded_for` has nothing to read and behind a reverse proxy every phone shares one client bucket (100/s, burst 300). From one address, between 3 and 6 of every 10 sessions never reached Loki while every SDK request was answered 200. Watch `ouo_sentry_translate_dropped_total{reason=~"consumer_.*"}` and `ouo_ratelimit_rejected_total`; fix in the receiver (copy `X-Forwarded-For` into `client.Info.Metadata`) or raise the client bucket for bridge traffic.
- **Spec-wire chunks from one address hit the same bucket**: 153 of 200 load chunks got 429 with the default limits. That is the limiter working; a load test from one machine has to raise `OUO_RATELIMIT_CLIENT_*` to measure anything else.
- **Loki's gRPC message limit breaks big reads.** Bridged mobile records carry about 1 KiB of structured metadata each; a 5 000-line query split is over Loki's default 4 MiB between querier and frontend (`received message larger than max (5306405 vs. 4194304)`), and the query hangs until its timeout (rum-api's session list, the plugin's Sessions page). `server.grpc_server_max_{recv,send}_msg_size` at 64 MiB fix it (the frontend's server side is the limit; the querier's client already allows 100 MiB, `/config?mode=defaults`, so do not set `frontend_worker.grpc_client_config` lower) (`deploy/compose/loki/config.yaml`, 2026-11-15): 60 s timeout to 1.0 s.
