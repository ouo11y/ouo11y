Sentry Android

Sentry Android copies the window with PixelCopy (or records a masking Canvas), walks the view and Compose trees for masks, keeps JPEGs on disk and encodes them with MediaCodec.

Builds on: Sentry iOS

Press next (or ▶ play all): the box doing the work lights up and the caption names the exact file and lines at a pinned release.
