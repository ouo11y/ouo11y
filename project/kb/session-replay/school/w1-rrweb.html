rrweb: the engine

rrweb is the open-source engine most web recorders are built on. It serialises the DOM with node ids, observes every change, and hands small events to the caller. It never uploads anything itself.

Builds on: The event loop and frames · Keyframes and deltas

The family tree: rrweb is the engine almost everyone uses. Sentry, PostHog and Highlight each ship their own fork of it and wrap it with buffering, compression and upload. Datadog, OpenReplay and Clarity wrote their own recorders with the same idea (serialise, observe, diff) and different encodings.

Press next (or ▶ play all): the box doing the work lights up and the caption names the exact file and lines at a pinned release.

The other half, rebuilding the page from these events, is lesson How to play.
