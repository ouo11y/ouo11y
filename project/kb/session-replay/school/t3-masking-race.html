How to mask: the masking race

Picture recorders mask by geometry, and geometry has a race: the tree walk and the pixel copy happen at slightly different moments. Here is the race, and the four defences vendors use against it.

Builds on: Masking: data versus geometry · View trees, platform by platform

A picture recorder walks the tree to learn where the private text is, and copies pixels to learn what is on screen. If the list scrolls between the two, the box lands on the wrong pixels. Every picture-based SDK has a defence; pick one and press ▶.

Each row is backed by the source links in the demo captions above.

Does Sentry drop frames in motion, or capture faster?

Neither. iOS keeps its rate during scrolling, waits (at most 1 s) while 4 or more animations run, and backs off to one frame per 2 s when a capture takes 150 ms or more. Android skips at most one frame that was drawn during the copy. Sentry trusts its timing; ouo refuses the frame. Neither has been measured for leaks: that is the masking-experiment session.
