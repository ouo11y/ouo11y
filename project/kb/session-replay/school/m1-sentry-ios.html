Sentry iOS

Sentry iOS takes masked screenshots about once a second, from a run-loop observer, and encodes every five seconds of them as an H.264 MP4 with an rrweb JSON sidecar.

Builds on: View trees, platform by platform · Compression basics, and how H.264 skips · How to mask: the masking race

Three camps: Sentry ships video (masked screenshots at 1 fps, H.264, one MP4 per 5 s). Datadog ships wireframes with a field-level diff and images sent once by hash. PostHog does both: wireframes (UIKit and Android Views) or one screenshot per changed frame, and forces screenshots on React Native and Flutter. OpenReplay sends JPEG screenshots in gzip archives. React Native is recorded by the native SDKs everywhere; Flutter is recorded in Dart everywhere.

Press next (or ▶ play all): the box doing the work lights up and the caption names the exact file and lines at a pinned release.
